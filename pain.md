## Rejected
- Make the marketplace free / undercut all paid packs — pricing complaint, not a verifiable trust gap; does not explain ClawHavoc or post-install tamper.
- Brand hate toward ClawHub / OpenClaw / Anthropic — narrative noise; evidence is about missing verification defaults, not “kill brand X”.
- Needs 50 engineers / full VirusTotal replacement — existing scanners (SkillSpector, ClawScan) already exist; pain is packaging scan+sign+benchmark into install UX and comparable rankings a small team can ship.
- “Just read every SKILL.md by hand” as the product — does not scale when directories claim 100k+ skills and VirusTotal-scale flagging is already public.
